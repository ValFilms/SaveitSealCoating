Our Services

Everything your asphalt needs in one local crew — from sealing fresh cracks to laying new pavement. Commercial & residential, always with a free estimate.

SERVICE 01

Crack Repair

Cracks let water seep beneath the surface, where freeze-and-thaw cycles widen them fast. We clean and fill cracks with hot rubber sealant that flexes with the pavement and locks water out.

  • Stops small cracks from spreading
  • Blocks water, the #1 cause of pavement failure
  • A smart, low-cost first step before sealcoating
Call for a Free Estimate
SERVICE 02

Paving

When a surface is past patching, fresh asphalt is the answer. We handle new installs and resurfacing for driveways, parking lots and private roads — properly graded for drainage and built to handle real traffic.

  • New driveways, lots & private roads
  • Resurfacing over worn-out asphalt
  • Proper grading for water runoff
Call for a Free Estimate
SERVICE 03

Sealcoating

Sealcoating is the maintenance step that makes everything else last. A fresh coat restores that deep black finish and shields asphalt from UV rays, rain, oil and gas — slowing the wear that leads to cracks and potholes.

  • Restores a rich, like-new black finish
  • Protects against sun, water, oil & gas
  • Extends the life of your pavement for years
Call for a Free Estimate
SERVICE 04

Pot Hole Repair

Potholes are more than an eyesore — they damage vehicles and create a liability on your property. We clean out and patch potholes with durable hot or cold mix, restoring a safe, smooth surface that holds up.

  • Fast, durable patching
  • Reduces trip & vehicle-damage liability
  • Keeps small failures from spreading
